# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Outpatient clinic nurses and practice managers — busy healthcare professionals handling high call volumes while also providing direct patient care. Secondary audience: physician owners and clinic administrators who control purchasing decisions. Context: a hectic shift environment, frequent interruptions, genuine frustration with hold times and missed callbacks. They are not technical buyers; they are practical ones.

## Product Purpose

PageMD is an AI-powered paging system for outpatient clinics. It answers every inbound call instantly, captures structured clinical intake (patient identity, request type, urgency), and routes prioritized, structured pages to the right provider — eliminating hold queues, missed callbacks, and the administrative burden that pulls nurses away from patients. Success means a clinic nurse who no longer dreads the phone.

## Brand Personality

Warm, Efficient, Human. Empathetic but no-nonsense. This is a tool for busy people who hate wasted time — it speaks to nurses and practice managers as real people, not as a product pitch. Confident without bravado. The tone earns trust through specificity, not superlatives.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS aesthetic (Intercom-style cream/purple gradients, "AI-powered" buzz copy, 2023-era startup template feel)
- Hyped AI aesthetic (glowing neural orbs, "the future is here" tone, excessive particle animations, dark-mode-with-neon)
- Corporate SaaS deck (rows of feature icons with one-line descriptions, heavy bullet lists, feels like a sales slide)
- Clinical/cold hospital portal (institutional white + teal, sterile, impersonal)

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The interactive demo is the core proof point. Every section should earn the viewer's trust through evidence, not adjectives.
2. **Specificity over hype.** "3 days remaining · Lisinopril 20 mg · Walgreens Main St." beats "AI-powered clinical intelligence." Concrete details are more persuasive than promises.
3. **Human technology.** AI is the mechanism, not the hero. Nurses and patients are the heroes. Design reflects that framing.
4. **One clear ask.** The page has a single conversion goal: Request Early Access. Every section leads toward it.
5. **Earned confidence.** The product is v1.0 entering pilot. Tone should be genuinely confident, not overbuilt. Understated assurance over startup exuberance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Audience includes healthcare professionals on a range of devices (desktop workstations, tablets at nurse stations, mobile on the go). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — already implemented. Touch targets appropriate for gloved or hasty interaction.
